(02) How it works

From search to keys in four steps.

Whether it's a scooter for the weekend or a car for a road trip, every rental on RentXe works the same way.

  1. 01 /

    Search

    Tell us where and when. Filter by vehicle type, brand, price and pick-up location.

  2. 02 /

    Compare

    See the same kind of vehicle from different companies, with prices, details and reviews.

  3. 03 /

    Request

    Send a booking request for your dates. The rental company approves it, and you can cancel free until 24 hours before pick-up.

  4. 04 /

    Ride & return

    Pick up, ride, and bring it back. The fee is worked out from your rental period, then leave a review.
