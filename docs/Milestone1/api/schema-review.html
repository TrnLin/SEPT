(03) Booking lifecycle

One booking, start to finish.

The seven statuses in the Booking model. Orange states block the vehicle's dates (SR-6, VEH-6). Only the first step has an endpoint so far; the dashed steps belong to pages with no mockup yet. Extending a rental keeps it PICKED_UP and moves its return date (VEH-38).

  1. 1
    Customer sends a request.POST /api/v1/bookings · vehicle.md · price stored at this moment (VEH-15)
  2. 2
    Company approves.No endpoint yet: company bookings page
  3. 3
    Company rejects.No endpoint yet
  4. 4
    Customer cancels.Free until 24 h before pick-up, then 50% of totalPrice (VEH-22, VEH-23). No endpoint yet: My bookings
  5. 5
    Company records pick-up.Deposit held. No endpoint yet (§2.5)
  6. 6
    Company records return.Condition, odometer, late fee (VEH-24, VEH-25). Booking has no condition field yet
  7. 7
    Customer writes a review.One per RETURNED booking. No endpoint yet
  8. 8
    Unanswered request expires.24 h after it was sent, or at the start of the pick-up day if sooner. A scheduled job; availability already skips it (VEH-36, decision 1)
  9. 9
    Customer extends the rental.From 7 days before return, up to 30 days at a time; the company approves. Stays PICKED_UP. No endpoint yet: My bookings (VEH-38)

Decision 3 · decided 6 Nov · SR-7, VEH-7

Does a booking's return day block the next pick-up?

The Tesla Model 3 is booked 11–12 Dec. Each row is a new request. Flip the switch to see which requests change if the return day becomes free.
