## Rubric: what a High Distinction needs

| Criterion | Pts | HD (80–100%) |
|---|---|---|
| Process and communication | 2.5 | All meetings professionally minuted (attendance, agenda, discussion summary, decisions, action items, follow-up). All issues correctly labelled. Clear, comprehensive Definition of Done. Board reflects current status. |
| Requirements and planning | 2.5 | All stories in WHO/WHAT/WHY format. All Sprint 1 stories follow INVEST, have realistic estimates and are split into actionable tasks. Backlog has at least 15 more well-defined stories. |
| SRS and design | 2.5 | All SRS sections complete and detailed. All architecture decisions documented and justified with rationale and impact analysis. Detailed wireframes for all UI views. |
| Testing | 2.5 | All Sprint 1 stories have clear acceptance criteria. Every criterion has a clear pass/fail. Criteria describe the outcome, not how it is achieved. |

Bands per criterion: HD > 1.98 · D > 1.73 · C > 1.48 · P > 1.23 · F ≤ 1.23.

Backlog stories beyond Sprint 1: HD 15+ · D 12+ · C 10+ · P 8+ · F 6 or fewer.
